## 导言 <!--block:lead-->

This project involved a 32-ton Rail-Mounted Container Gantry Crane exported to Russia, used primarily for loading, unloading, and transferring containers. Given the extremely cold local climate, the main structure is fabricated from Q355E low-temperature-resistant steel. The electrical system was specially engineered for cold resistance and equipped with a comprehensive safety protection system, enabling stable operation across an ambient temperature range of -40℃ to +40℃.

## 核心采购要求正文 <!--block:req-->

The Russian customer's core requirement was not simply to "buy a gantry crane capable of lifting containers," but to obtain a complete, reliable solution—one that could operate stably in an extremely low-temperature environment, minimize downtime, and adapt to local operating systems.

The customer's needs and requirements included:

- There were concerns that the equipment might freeze, become brittle, or fail to start at -40°C. A Rail-Mounted Container Gantry Crane capable of reliable operation at -40°C was therefore required, for long-term outdoor use in Russia's cold regions.
- Because a mismatch with local operating habits and electrical standards could affect operation and maintenance, the control system needed Russian-language labeling and compliance with local power-supply standards, for more convenient and efficient operation and management.
- Safety risks and potential faults exist over long-term operation, so a comprehensive safety protection system was required.
- Because critical components could be damaged during long-distance transport, affecting installation and commissioning, a reliable packaging solution was needed to ensure safe delivery.

Based on these considerations, we developed a fully targeted system design and packaging solution.

## 低温适应性设计正文 <!--block:lowtemp-->

To ensure reliable operation in Russia's -40°C low-temperature environment, a dedicated low-temperature design was adopted:

- **Q355E low-temperature steel**: The main structure uses Q355E steel plate, which retains excellent impact toughness at -40°C, effectively preventing brittle fracture and ensuring safe, reliable operation in cold environments.
- **Cold-resistant cabling**: The entire crane uses cold-resistant cables that remain flexible and maintain insulation performance in cold conditions, preventing cable hardening, cracking, and signal instability, thereby improving electrical reliability.
- **Motor heating system**: Both the hoisting and travel motors are fitted with heating tapes for preheating and heat retention in low-temperature environments. This prevents hard starting and mechanical seizure, ensuring stable operation.
- **Insulated cabin**: The operator cabin offers good thermal insulation and is equipped with air conditioning. The temperature adjusts automatically to ambient conditions, ensuring operator comfort.

## 本地化设计正文 <!--block:localization-->

To ensure ease of operation and local compatibility, the system was optimized for the Russian market:

- **Russian-language labeling**: The video monitoring system and cabin console are fitted with bilingual English and Russian labels, allowing local operators to easily identify functions and equipment status, reducing the risk of misoperation and improving maintenance efficiency.
- **Local voltage compatibility**: The equipment is designed for a 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase power supply, fully compliant with Russian industrial standards to ensure direct installation and stable operation.

## 安全保护系统正文 <!--block:safety-->

A comprehensive safety protection system was configured to ensure safe and stable operation:

- **Overload protection system**: Real-time load monitoring with multi-level alarms and motion limiting to prevent structural overload.
- **Dual limit protection**: Hoisting and travel limit switches prevent over-travel and mechanical damage.
- **Emergency braking system**: Emergency-stop buttons are provided on all four legs, with a system-wide emergency-stop function for rapid shutdown in emergencies.
- **Anti-wind system**: Wind-speed sensors are integrated with a rail-clamping system; when wind speed exceeds the safe limit, it automatically alarms and locks onto the rail to prevent slippage.
- **Electrical protection system**: Includes over-current, over-temperature, phase-loss, phase-sequence-error, under-voltage, and grounding protection to reduce the risk of electrical faults.

## 运输保护设计正文 <!--block:transport-->

Given the long-distance transport to Russia, all critical components were specially protected:

- The trolley is fully wrapped in tarpaulin to prevent damage from rain and moisture. The hoisting motor and gearbox motor are secured with shock-absorbing foam to reduce vibration damage.
- The travel mechanism's gearbox motor was first reinforced and secured with shock-absorbing foam to ensure stability during transport. It was then covered with a layer of waterproof tarpaulin to enhance moisture and rain resistance.
- The insulated operator's cab and cable reel were packed in wooden crates to enhance impact resistance and prevent damage during transport.

This ensures that the equipment arrives on site in a condition close to its factory-delivery state.

## 交付结果正文 <!--block:delivery-->

The delivered 32-ton Rail-Mounted Container Gantry Crane successfully met all of the customer's key requirements:

- Stable operation in a -40°C low-temperature environment
- Full compliance with local Russian electrical and operating standards
- Comprehensive outdoor safety protection system
- Critical components in good condition after transport, ensuring smooth on-site installation.

The crane was customized to the project requirements, with the following specifications:

- **Lifting capacity**: 32 tons (under the spreader)
- **Spreader**: 40 ft container spreader (supports rotation, translation, hoisting, and container-handling operations)
- **Span**: 20 m
- **Lifting height**: 10 m (under the spreader)
- **Load class**: ISO M6
- **Operating temperature**: -40°C to +40°C
- **Power Supply**: 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase

The project achieved unified performance in low-temperature adaptability, system compatibility, and transport reliability. All of the key risks the customer was initially concerned about were addressed during the design and manufacturing stages, and the equipment operated stably after commissioning.

## 项目概要正文 <!--block:summary-->

The core of this project was not merely to deliver a gantry crane, but to provide a complete, reliable container-handling solution for an extremely cold environment.

By selecting low-temperature materials, reinforcing the electrical system, and applying localized design and transport protection, the equipment is not only "technically compliant" but also "reliable in long-term operation under real-world conditions."
